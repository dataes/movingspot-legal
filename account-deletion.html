Account Deletion

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Delete your MovingSpot account

You can permanently delete your MovingSpot account directly from the mobile app.

Go to:

Profile → Delete account

Confirm the deletion when prompted.

In certain situations, additional assistance from support may be required to complete the deletion process.

What happens when your account is deleted

Account deletion removes your account and profile.

Associated active information such as current presence, current vibe activity, chat messages, notification information, block relationships and profile images is also removed as part of the deletion process.

Places created by your account may be deactivated.

Events you previously created may remain available, but your account will no longer be identified as their creator.

Certain moderation, support, security or deletion-operation information may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to MovingSpot, you can request account deletion by contacting:

support@movingspot.app

Where possible, send the request from the email address associated with your MovingSpot account.

Suggested subject:

Delete my MovingSpot account

We may need to verify that you own the account before processing the request.
