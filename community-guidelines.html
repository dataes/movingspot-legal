Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 18, 2026

MovingSpot is about discovering what is happening around you, sharing the vibe and connecting around places and events.

Everyone helps keep that experience useful, welcoming and safe.

Be respectful

Treat other users with respect.

Do not harass, threaten, intimidate or deliberately target others.

Hate speech, discrimination and abusive behaviour are not welcome on MovingSpot.

Keep it real

Share genuine information about places, events and the current vibe.

Do not:

  • deliberately create fake events;
  • knowingly provide false place information;
  • manipulate presence or vibe information;
  • impersonate another person;
  • spam chats;
  • use MovingSpot for scams or deceptive behaviour.

Mistakes happen. Deliberately misleading others is different.

Respect privacy

Do not share another person's private or sensitive information without permission.

Do not use MovingSpot to stalk, expose or endanger someone.

Someone being present at a place does not give others permission to share additional private information about them.

Respect intellectual property

Share only content that you created yourself or are authorised to use.

Do not copy descriptions, photographs, artwork, flyers, logos or other protected content from another website, platform or service unless you have permission to use it.

Keep chat safe

Do not use chat for:

  • harassment or threats;
  • hate speech;
  • unwanted sexual content;
  • scams or spam;
  • illegal activity;
  • content intended to harm or intimidate others.

Report

If a chat message violates these Guidelines:

Press and hold the message → Report

Explain why you are reporting it and submit the report.

For an event or other content, use Feedback in MovingSpot or email support@movingspot.app.

Reports may be reviewed by an authorised moderator.

Reported content may be removed when it violates these Guidelines.

Block

You can block another user through the Network blocking functionality where available.

Blocking hides that user's chat messages and reactions from you and prevents certain future notifications generated by that user from being sent to you.

Blocking does not make the user completely invisible throughout MovingSpot.

You can unblock them later.

Protect children

Any sexual exploitation or abuse of children is strictly prohibited.

This includes child sexual abuse material, grooming, sextortion and attempts to sexually exploit a minor.

Enforcement

Content that violates these Guidelines may be removed.

Serious violations may result in further action against the responsible account.

Contact

For safety concerns:

support@movingspot.app
