Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 18, 2026

MovingSpot has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

These standards apply to content and behaviour on MovingSpot.

Prohibited behaviour

MovingSpot prohibits:

  • child sexual abuse or exploitation;
  • child sexual abuse material (CSAM);
  • grooming or attempts to sexually exploit a minor;
  • sextortion involving minors;
  • sexualised content involving minors;
  • requesting, sharing or distributing sexual content involving minors;
  • using MovingSpot to target a minor for sexual purposes.

Reporting concerns

If a concern involves a chat message:

Press and hold the message → Report

Explain the concern and submit the report.

For other child-safety concerns, contact:

support@movingspot.app

How we respond

When MovingSpot becomes aware of content or behaviour involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, appropriate action may be taken.

This may include removing relevant content, taking action against the responsible account, preserving information where legally required and reporting illegal material or conduct to competent authorities where required by applicable law.

Child safety and events

MovingSpot does not determine the age restrictions applicable to individual venues or events.

Places and events may have their own age restrictions, and users are responsible for complying with them.
