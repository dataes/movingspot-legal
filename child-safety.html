Child Safety Standards for MovingSpot: Brussels

Last updated: September 22, 2026

MovingSpot: Brussels is published on Google Play by dataes and operated by IRD (Inoca Research And Development).

MovingSpot: Brussels explicitly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

These published standards apply to all content and behaviour on MovingSpot.

Prohibited behaviour

MovingSpot prohibits:

  • child sexual abuse or exploitation;
  • child sexual abuse material (CSAM);
  • grooming or attempts to sexually exploit a minor;
  • sextortion involving minors;
  • sexualised content involving minors;
  • requesting, sharing or distributing sexual content involving minors;
  • using MovingSpot to target a minor for sexual purposes.

Reporting concerns

If a concern involves a chat message:

Press and hold the message → Report

Explain the concern and submit the report.

For any other child-safety concern, contact the MovingSpot Child Safety Team:

support@movingspot.app

How we respond

When MovingSpot becomes aware of content or behaviour involving CSAE or CSAM, we take appropriate action in accordance with these standards and applicable law.

This includes removing prohibited content after obtaining actual knowledge of it, taking action against the responsible account, preserving relevant information where legally required and reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant regional authority as required by applicable law.

Compliance with child safety laws

MovingSpot complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations and cooperates with competent authorities where legally required.

Child safety and events

MovingSpot does not determine the age restrictions applicable to individual venues or events.

Places and events may have their own age restrictions, and users are responsible for complying with them.
