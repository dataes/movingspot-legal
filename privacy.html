Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MovingSpot processes personal information when you use the service.

MovingSpot is operated by Inoca Research And Development (IRD).

1. Information we process

Account information

MovingSpot uses your email address to create and authenticate your account.

Profile information

Your profile may include:

  • a pseudonym;
  • an optional profile picture;
  • your selected age range;
  • your selected gender response;
  • a self-description if you select that option.

You may choose Prefer not to say for age and gender.

Age and gender information is not displayed to other users.

Content you provide

MovingSpot may process content you submit, including:

  • events;
  • places;
  • chat messages;
  • photos and videos shared through chat;
  • check-ins and presence information;
  • vibe information;
  • reports;
  • feedback and support requests.

2. Location

MovingSpot may temporarily access your device location when you actively use a location-based feature, such as finding or checking into a nearby place or event.

MovingSpot does not continuously track your location in the background.

Device coordinates used to perform a check-in are not stored as part of your presence record.

Other users may see the place where you are currently present, but they do not receive your device's precise GPS coordinates.

3. Presence and vibe

When you check in, MovingSpot processes the place where you choose to indicate your presence and the period during which it remains active.

You may also submit information about the current vibe of a place.

Current presence and vibe information is temporary.

4. Chat

MovingSpot provides place-based chat containing text, photos and short videos.

Chat messages are intended to be temporary and are removed when the associated place no longer has active presence, subject to moderation and operational requirements.

If a message is reported, information relating to the report may remain available separately for moderation purposes after the original message disappears.

5. Reports

When you report content, MovingSpot may process:

  • information identifying the reporter and the author of the reported message;
  • information about the reported message;
  • the reason you provide;
  • the relevant place;
  • timestamps;
  • information about how the report was handled.

This information is used for moderation, safety and abuse prevention.

6. Blocking

When you block another user, MovingSpot processes information identifying:

  • your account;
  • the blocked account;
  • when the block was created.

A reason for blocking is not stored as part of the block itself.

The blocked user is not directly notified.

Block information is used to hide that user's chat messages and reactions from you and to suppress certain future notifications generated by that user.

Block relationships are removed when you unblock the person or when either associated account is permanently deleted.

7. Notifications

If you enable notifications, MovingSpot processes the information necessary to deliver them.

Notification preferences can be managed within MovingSpot and through your device settings.

Blocking another user also prevents certain future message, presence and event notifications generated by that person from being sent to you.

8. Age and gender statistics

MovingSpot may use age-range, gender, presence and activity information to produce protected aggregate statistics.

These statistics are intended to describe groups and trends rather than display individual demographic information to other users.

9. Why we process information

We process personal information where necessary to:

  • create and authenticate accounts;
  • provide MovingSpot;
  • display events and places;
  • provide location-based features;
  • manage presence and vibe information;
  • operate chat;
  • deliver notifications;
  • apply user blocks;
  • review reports and moderate content;
  • produce aggregated service statistics;
  • provide support;
  • maintain security and prevent abuse;
  • comply with legal obligations.

Depending on the processing involved, the legal basis may include performance of our agreement with you, our legitimate interests, your consent where required, or compliance with legal obligations.

10. Service providers and recipients

MovingSpot uses trusted technical service providers where necessary to operate the service, including providers involved in infrastructure, authentication, data storage, notifications and location functionality.

These providers may process information only where necessary to provide their services and subject to applicable data-protection requirements.

MovingSpot does not sell your personal information.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate serious abuse or establish or defend legal claims.

11. International processing

Some technical service providers may process personal information outside Belgium or the European Economic Area.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers.

12. Retention

Different information is retained for different periods depending on its purpose.

In particular:

  • current presence expires according to the selected duration or when you check out;
  • current vibe information is temporary;
  • place-chat messages are designed to disappear when the relevant place no longer has active presence;
  • blocks remain until you unblock the person or either associated account is deleted;
  • account and profile information generally remains while your account is active;
  • reports and moderation, support or security information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

13. Account deletion

You can initiate deletion of your MovingSpot account directly from your profile.

Account deletion removes your account and profile and data associated with active service features, subject to limited exceptions.

Events that you previously created may remain available without identifying you as their creator.

Certain moderation, support, security or operational information may be retained where reasonably necessary or legally required.

See the Account Deletion page for more information.

14. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • access your personal information;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • request deletion;
  • restrict certain processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • receive certain information in a portable format where applicable;
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To exercise your rights, contact:

support@movingspot.app

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority.

15. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as MovingSpot, applicable law or our practices evolve.

The current version will always display its actual last-updated date.
