9. Blocking

MovingSpot allows you to block another user.

The blocked user is not directly notified that you blocked them.

Each person controls only their own block. A block is removed when its owner unblocks or when either associated account is permanently deleted. Direct content becomes visible again only after neither person blocks the other.
